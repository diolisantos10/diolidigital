Adult content
Last updated: August 2026
Principle

At TikTok, we are committed to maintaining a safe and positive environment for our community We aim to make our ad content appropriate for everyone. Therefore, as an advertiser, it is your responsibility to follow all advertising regulations, laws, and standards for the promotion of adult sexual products, services, dating services, entertainment, and depictions of sexual content.

Policy

The following sections outline our Adult Content policy by category:

Dating apps

Live chat

Sexual services

Sexual products

Sexually explicit content

Sexually suggestive content

Dating apps

Dating app ads must not feature content that is discriminatory, exploitative, dishonest, or sexually inappropriate. Ads or landing pages that promote dating apps that are purely designed for casual sex are not allowed. In markets where dating app ads are allowed*, the following requirements must be met:

Ads must be restricted to audiences aged 18 years and older

You must work with a TikTok Sales Representative

The landing page must be a Google Play, Apple App Store, or a website landing page.

Examples of what is not allowed:

Portraying single life as a negative lifestyle

Models that appear to be 21 years of age or younger

References to adult or sexual services or products

Sexually explicit language or content

Sexually suggestive text, audio, or content

Explicit exposure of breasts, buttocks, or genitalia

Provocative body language

Excessive skin exposure

Infidelity

International brides or mail-order brides

Exchange in terms of income or compensation including goods or services

Sugar dating, or any type of engagement in companionship or relationships in exchange for compensation or material benefit

*Dating app ads are not allowed in the following markets: Bahrain, Iraq, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Morocco, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia

Live chat apps

In markets where live chat app ads are allowed*, the following requirements must be met:

Ads must be restricted to audiences aged 18 years and older

You must work with a TikTok Sales Representative to:

Obtain our explicit permission

Make sure other requirements are met

Sexual services

Sexual services are not allowed.

Examples of what is not allowed:

Adult entertainment, such as host or hostess clubs, strip clubs, escort services, prostitution, or erotic massages

Sexual chats, imagery, and pornography

Member exclusive content and streaming of adult content through a webcam, such as stripping, nude modeling, and masturbation (sexcamming)

Services that create content for sexual pleasure or sexual intention purposes, such as AI Nudify Apps

Sexual products

Sexual products are not allowed.

Examples of what is not allowed:

Pornographic material, sex toys, and supplies such as fetish or sexual fantasy costumes

Promotion, sale, solicitation, or facilitation of access to the enhancement of sexual performance, pleasure, or bodily appearance, such as drugs, medications, tools, or devices such as penis enlargement or breast enhancement products or services

Sexually explicit content

Sexually explicit content is not allowed.

Examples of what is not allowed:

Penetrative sex, non-penetrative sex, and oral sex

Fetish and kink activity

Displaying nudity

Sexually explicit text, audio or term that directly describes or references specific sexual activity, body parts, or sexual products

Sexually suggestive content

Sexually suggestive content is not allowed.

Examples of what is not allowed:

Overtly sexual kissing

Sexually suggestive text or audio

Sexualized body positions

Emphasis on sensitive body parts or sensitive regions

Flirtatious movements

Stripping or undressing

Swimsuits, lingerie, or clothing that exposes or implies exposure of intimate areas

Imitating sexual positions or sexual acts

Promote on TikTok

In the following markets, Promote is the only form of paid ads that is allowed, but dating apps and live chat app ads are not allowed:

Albania, Brunei, El Salvador, Ethiopia, Honduras, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Libya, Mali, Mauritius, Monaco, Myanmar, Nicaragua, Senegal, Tanzania, Uganda
