Branded Content Policy - Market-specific Requirements
Last updated: November 2025

In addition to the Branded Content restrictions outlined in our global Branded Content Policy, including our global prohibition on political content, Branded Content featuring or related to the product or service categories below is either prohibited or will be restricted in the applicable markets. If you create this Branded Content, we will prevent it from being accessed in the relevant markets or displayed to the relevant audiences.

Our Branded Content and TikTok Ads policies differ. Content that is not allowed in Branded Content may still be allowed in TikTok Ads. If you would like to promote your content in TikTok Ads, we recommend understanding and complying with our TikTok Advertising Policies.

Energy drinks

Energy drinks are not allowed in Bangladesh

Film, TV shows, and game trailers

Film, TV shows, and game trailers are allowed without age restrictions in the following markets:

Bangladesh, Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Vietnam, Singapore

All film, TV shows, and game trailers will be restricted to audiences aged 18 years and older no matter their age rating in the following markets:

Argentina, Austria, Bahrain, Belgium, Brazil, Bulgaria, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Egypt, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Guatemala, Hungary, Iceland, Iraq, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Jordan, Kuwait, Latvia, Lebanon, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Mexico, Morocco, Netherlands, Norway, Oman, Panama, Peru, Poland, Portugal, Qatar, Romania, Saudi Arabia, Slovakia, Slovenia, South Africa, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, Uruguay

Film, TV show, and game trailers must show the age rating of the movie, TV show, or game within the content and will be restricted to audiences according to that rating in the following markets:

Australia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, New Zealand, Ukraine

Film, TV show, and game trailers rated for audiences aged 14 years and older will be restricted to audiences aged 18 years and older no matter their age rating in the following markets:

Australia, Canada*, New Zealand, United States*

*Note: If the video or caption does not clearly include the film, TV show, or game trailer's age rating in Canada or the United States, it will be restricted to audiences aged 18 years and older.

Film, TV show, and game trailers rated for audiences over the age of 18 years are not allowed in the following markets:

Japan, South Korea, Taiwan

Film, TV show, and game trailers that reference adult content or themes are not allowed in the following markets:

Japan

Food and beverages

All food and beverages are not allowed in Kazakhstan

Raw food or beverages are not allowed in Vietnam

Food and beverages must respect and adapt to local culture and religious restrictions in the following markets:

Bahrain, Egypt, Iraq, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Morocco, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and United Arab Emirates

Pork food products are not allowed in Bangladesh

High in saturated fat, salt, and sugar (HFSS)

Food or drinks classified as high in fat, salt, or sugar (HFSS) are not allowed in the following markets:

Ireland, Norway, Portugal, United Kingdom

Weight loss products or services

Waist trainers and face and body sculpting products are not allowed in the following markets:

Belarus and Kazakstan

Products or services designed for minors

Products or services designed for minors, including toys, children's clothing, diapers, baby products, infant food, tutoring services, and summer camps are allowed, but will be restricted to audiences aged 18 years and older in the following markets:

Australia, Argentina, Brazil, Canada, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Guatemala, Mexico, New Zealand, Panama, Peru, United States, Uruguay

Branded Content promoting products or services designed for minors is not allowed to speak to or exhort minors, or appear overly childish in an attempt to appeal to minors in the following markets:

Australia, Argentina, Brazil, Canada, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Guatemala, Mexico, New Zealand, Panama, Peru, United States, Uruguay

Professional services

Real estate and real estate services are not allowed in Belarus, Kazakhstan, and Ukraine

Employment services are not allowed in Belarus and Kazakhstan

Overseas medical institutions, financial services, and real estate services are not allowed in Japan

Religious content

All religious content is not allowed in the following markets:

Japan, South Korea, Taiwan

Underwear

Underwear is not allowed to be shown on a model in the following markets:

South Korea, Taiwan
